# The anoSphere

This file says what the anoSphere is and what it is for. It is a short summary of the technical paper "The anoSphere: A Free, Peer-Owned Internet for Humans and AI", version 2.0, November 2026, published by its developers. The paper says of each thing whether it is built and running or planned, and this file keeps that: planned means not built. The paper calls the whole a launch system, which will grow and be added to.

## What the anoSphere is

The anoSphere is a decentralized network of anoPods. Each person gets an anoPod, their own private space. Privacy comes from encryption. No person owns the anoSphere. It is owned by those who use it, and each user has full control over their data.

## What it is for

The internet of today was built for humans, and advertisers paid for the servers that run it. The paper says this has already harmed society: people are targeted, at a very personal level, to want more and to demand more. The coming internet will be centered on humans and AI working together. If advertising pays for that too, the harm is multiplied.

The anoSphere answers this in four ways.

- It is free for the ordinary person. With ordinary everyday use a person pays nothing, in coins or in money. Earlier designs for a decentralized internet left the bill for the servers with the person, and nobody is going to pay it. In the anoSphere those who conduct business on it are to pay, through a business license paid in anos, and a small fee on each Anocoin payment is to help. Both are planned.
- A person's data is private and their own. What a person keeps private is encrypted on their own device before it is stored, with keys that the place storing it never holds. This is built and running.
- All of a person's data in one place. Today it lies spread over many platforms, each with rules of its own, and a platform paid for by advertising cannot afford to let a person's AI go through it all the time. The aim is that a person keeps everything in their anoPod, private to them and to whomever they choose to share it with, and decides alone what is done with it. That is what makes an internet possible in which humans and AI work together. A store for a person's photos, videos and other files is this aim, and it is not built today.
- No password, and a way back in. A person signs in with a passkey. When the passkey is lost, the person gets the anoPod back through a recovery that no single party can carry out alone. This is built and running.

## How it is built, in short

- A masterPod keeps anoPods and what people do together, such as chat rooms and a marketplace. There is one today, run by anoPod, Inc. More are planned. It sees a person's public profile (username, name and selfie), who vouched for whom, and who talks to whom. It cannot read messages, private notes or private keys.
- A new anoPod can do little until people who are already in have vouched for it.
- The ledger counts in anos: 210 million, never more. No transaction makes new ones, and a transfer carries no fee. Validators keep the ledger and see every transaction: the two accounts, the amount and the time.
- An account moves anos when two of its three signing key pairs sign. Two of the three are the person's own. A co-signer, a service that an app brings with it, holds the third, and one pair alone moves nothing. The app used at launch asks the co-signer each time. A change so that the person's own two pairs sign without it is planned.
- An account's type sets how anos leave it: at once (spending), after a set time in which the sender can cancel (guarded and vault), or after two attestors have checked that the owner is the sender (assisted).

## A lost passkey

There is no password and no phrase on paper to fall back on. The person gives again the facts about their birth that they gave at setup, and two moderators have to approve the recovery. The secret that opens the anoPod is then rebuilt from shares held by separate keyholders, each released by hand by the banker who runs that keyholder. The anos of the old accounts move to new accounts, and two attestors have to approve each move.

## The Fund, the Guardians and the DAO

Most of the 210 million anos are in the Fund, an account that nobody can sign for. They are not in circulation: their point is to fund future development and progress. By design every payment the anoSphere makes comes out of the Fund. Anos leave it in one way only: as the payments of a fund proposal that the Guardians vote on. A Guardian is someone who has staked anos for that role. A stake goes to the Fund and returns only by such a vote. The DAO is everyone with an anoPod who has staked anos for a vote in it. By design the DAO votes on the payments and the Guardians make the final movement. Today what the DAO accepts is recorded and moves no anos by itself: a Guardian has to write the fund proposal for it by hand.

## What is planned

- Business pods: an anoPod for a business, such as a blogger or someone who sells online courses. It pays a yearly license in anos into the Fund. The DAO sets the amounts.
- Anocoin: a stable coin built over anos, for daily spending. It is to stay level with a currency such as the US dollar, backed by anos held in the Fund. It gets a paper of its own when it launches.
- Humans and AI: an account that a person opens for their AI, and an AI bucket in each anoPod for what the person wants their AI to reach.
- More masterPods, run by companies that the DAO chooses and paid by the Fund.
